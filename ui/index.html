How to read this

Kronos draws many possible price paths for the candles ahead. The solid line is the median path; the dashed lines mark where 80% of paths fell.

  • A median near 0% inside a wide band means the model sees no edge.
  • "Paths ending higher" near 50% is a coin flip. Look for a lopsided split that holds on more than one interval.
  • The model uses price and volume only. It knows nothing about news, funding or the order book.
  • More paths give a steadier range. The forecast starts after the last closed candle.
  • Stocks, forex and futures only forecast trading hours: nights, weekends and lunch breaks are skipped. Under a day, "next 4h" means the next 4 hours the market is open; "next 1w" means 5 sessions. Times are the exchange's local time.
